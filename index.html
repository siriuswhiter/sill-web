01

往上一放Drop it upward

文件、图片、文字或链接，拖到屏幕顶部就进岛；也可在岛上按 ⌘V 手动粘贴。Pro 还可用 ⌥⌘C 从任意位置一键抓取剪贴板。Files, images, text, or links go straight to the island, and you can press ⌘V on the island to paste manually. Pro also captures the clipboard from anywhere with ⌥⌘C.

02

安心留在本机Keep it on your Mac

内容留在本机，单项删除可立即撤销，也可在“最近删除”中永久清除。Pro 增加固定长存、自动清理、7 天历史恢复，以及通过 Touch ID 或系统密码保护的敏感内容。Everything stays on your Mac. A single deletion can be undone immediately or permanently removed from Recently Deleted. Pro adds pinning, scheduled cleanup, 7-day restore history, and sensitive-item protection with Touch ID or your Mac password.

03

需要时拿走Take it when needed

从岛上直接拖进邮件、聊天或编辑器，也可双击复制。查看、打开、复制、拖出、重新定位引用文件和删除已有内容始终可用。Drag straight into Mail, chat, or your editor, or double-click to copy. Existing items always remain viewable, openable, copyable, draggable, relocatable, and deletable.

Sill natively supports 4 kinds: files, images, text snippets, and web URLs. To retrieve, simply drag the tile directly from the island into your target application (Mail, Slack, Xcode, Figma), or double-click to copy it to your clipboard.

不会上传您的暂存内容，也不会持续轮询剪贴板。Free 仅在您聚焦置物岛并按 ⌘V 时读取一次；Pro 还可通过 ⌥⌘C 或抓取菜单主动触发。配置了诊断服务的发布版本可能发送不含暂存内容的最小化崩溃与性能信息，详见隐私协议。
