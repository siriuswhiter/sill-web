还有些小问题？A few things to know.

关于兼容性、隐私和购买。Compatibility, privacy, and purchasing.

没有硬件刘海的 Mac（如 Mac mini、Studio、iMac 或外接显示器）可以使用吗？ Does Sill work on Macs without a notch (Mac mini, Studio, external monitors)?

完全支持。无刘海屏幕会自动使用四角圆润的悬浮形态，Free 与 Pro 都能正常收起、展开、拖放和取回内容。Pro 还可以在有刘海的 Mac 上手动切换“融合刘海”与“无刘海模式”。

Absolutely. Displays without a notch automatically use the rounded floating shape, and both Free and Pro support the same collapse, expand, drop, and retrieval workflow. Pro also lets you manually switch between Notch Blend and Notch-free on a notched Mac.

拖入暂存的大文件会占用两倍 Mac 硬盘空间吗？ Will dragging large files duplicate disk usage on my Mac?

大文件默认引用原位置，避免重复占用空间；小文件会保存一份本地副本。Pro 用户将引用文件固定长存时，Sill 也会创建本地副本。

Large files are referenced in place to avoid duplication; small files are copied into local storage. Pinning a referenced file with Pro also creates a local copy.

支持拖入哪些类型的内容？怎么取回到其他应用？ What content kinds are supported? How do I retrieve them?

Sill 原生支持 4 类内容：文件（PDF/DMG/Office 等）、图片（PNG/JPG/WebP 等）、随手划选的文本段落与网址链接。取回时，只需从置物岛中将磁贴直接拖出放入目标窗口（如邮件附件区、微信对话框或设计稿），或者双击磁贴直接复制到剪贴板。

Sill natively supports 4 kinds: files, images, text snippets, and web URLs. To retrieve, simply drag the tile directly from the island into your target application (Mail, Slack, Xcode, Figma), or double-click to copy it to your clipboard.

Sill 会在后台偷窥剪贴板或上传数据吗？ Does Sill silently monitor clipboard or upload my data?

不会上传您的暂存内容，也不会持续轮询剪贴板。Free 仅在您聚焦置物岛并按 ⌘V 时读取一次；Pro 还可通过 ⌥⌘C 或抓取菜单主动触发。配置了诊断服务的发布版本可能发送不含暂存内容的最小化崩溃与性能信息，详见隐私协议。

Sill never uploads your stashed content or continuously polls the clipboard. Free reads it once when you focus the island and press ⌘V; Pro can also trigger a read with ⌥⌘C or the capture menu. Release builds configured for diagnostics may send minimal crash and performance information without stashed content; see the Privacy Policy.

Sill Pro 是订阅还是一次买断？ Is Sill Pro a subscription or a one-time purchase?

Sill Pro 是一次性非消耗型购买，不收取月费或年费；购买与通过 Apple 恢复购买无需 Sill 账号。可选择通过 Apple 或 Google 创建 Sill 账号，免费体验 Pro 7 天。实际价格与购买条款以 App 内显示为准。

Sill Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase with no monthly or annual fee. Buying and restoring through Apple do not require a Sill account. You can optionally create one with Apple or Google to try Pro free for 7 days. Local pricing and purchase terms are shown in the app.

如果 Pro 权益暂时不可用，我的内容和设置会怎样？ What happens if Pro access is temporarily unavailable?

您的内容、标签和设置不会被删除，即使当前已有内容超过 Free 的 5 项上限也会全部保留，只会暂停继续添加。运行时会回退到默认置物岛，搜索、编辑、整理、恢复和自动化暂停，但已有内容仍可查看、打开、复制、拖出、重新定位和删除。已经加密的敏感内容仍可验证后查看并随时取消保护。

Your content, tags, and settings are never deleted. Even if you already exceed Free's 5-item limit, everything remains and only new additions pause. Runtime falls back to the default island; search, editing, organization, restore, and automation pause, while existing items remain viewable, openable, copyable, draggable, relocatable, and deletable. Existing encrypted items remain viewable after authentication and can always have protection removed.
