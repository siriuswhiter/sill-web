SILL FIELD NOTE · 001

Not a cat. Not a dragon.
He is Pobb.

A little liquid-scaled creature who lives along the lower rim of Sill. While you drop things, pick them up, and move on, he stays behind to watch over the moments that do not have a home yet.

SpeciesTidecore
Height0.4 m
Weight8.7 kg
HabitatLower rim

Softer than he looks, heavier than you expect.
He keeps quiet watch here.

01 / ORIGIN

Born from a tide that forgot to leave

When the tide withdraws, one drop of water will sometimes refuse to return to the sea. It hides in a warm seam of stone and slowly grows weight, a heartbeat, and a broad tail. Much later, the drop opens its eyes—and a tidecore is born.

Beneath Pobb's deep navy coat is a water-core that can flow and gather itself again. That is why a poke makes him squash before springing back like a full drop of water. And when he needs to travel, he tucks in his short paws and rolls a little way along the island floor.

02 / FOUR SCALES

Four scales, four things left unsaid

They are not decoration, and they are not wings. Every tidecore carries exactly four liquid-glass scales, descending from shoulder to tail.

01 · SENSE

Sensing

The top scale turns first toward cursors, sounds, and newly arrived things—a quiet little radar.

02 · SHELTER

Shelter

At bedtime the four scales fold forward, one by one, into an aqua blanket that just covers his face.

03 · MEMORY

Memory

Their water-light briefly keeps nearby colors. Pobb never reads your things; he only remembers that you placed something here with care.

04 · FEELING

Feeling

They glow tail-to-shoulder when happy, flare together when startled, and breathe like still water when he feels safe.

03 / TEMPERAMENT

Slow to trust, quick to notice, quietly stubborn

78%

Quiet curiosity

He never rushes toward something new. First the ears, then the eyes, then the top scale quietly turns.

91%

Steady company

He never asks you to organize or explain. He simply stays nearby so the things you set down do not feel abandoned.

64%

Measured affection

One gentle poke is welcome. He tolerates a second. On the third, he becomes a ball and rolls just out of reach.

LIKES

  • Rain ticking against glass
  • Small things placed with care
  • The first blue light of morning
  • The quiet second after a success sound
  • When someone pauses to notice him

AVOIDS

  • A cursor diving in too fast
  • Too many things falling at once
  • Heights, hovering, and nowhere to land
  • Being called a hamster (he understands)
  • Having his scales counted while asleep

A SMALL AGREEMENT

You do not need to keep him company.
