2.0

One Island, several ways to use it.

Tools

Apps and Files open applications and browse folders. Shelf, Note, Clipboard, and Translate help you work with content and text.

Explore tools

Components

Clock, Weather, Pomodoro, and Photo can stay on the Island, offering a useful glance or a short interaction when needed.

Explore components

Companions and scenes

Pobb, Professor Aubie, living backgrounds, and World Landmarks give the Island a life beyond controls. World Landmarks is included with Pro and an active trial.

Free / Pro

Free includes Apps, Files, Clock, Weather, and basic settings. Pro is a one-time purchase for content tools, more components, OCR, sensitive-content protection, and backgrounds.
