A few things
before you start.

What can I do with Sill?

Browse tools and components in the main window. Use Apps and Files to launch apps and browse folders, and keep Clock, Weather, or other components on your Island. Pro adds Shelf, Note, Clipboard, Translate, Pomodoro, Photo, and companions.

What is included with Free?

Free includes Apps and Files tools, Clock and Weather components, and basic Island appearance, layout, shortcut, and display-placement settings. Pro adds unlimited Shelf, Note, Clipboard history, Translate, Pomodoro, Photo, companions, OCR search, sensitive-content protection, included living backgrounds, and custom image backgrounds.

Is the race the app itself?

The rainbow race is an interactive website demo showing how different content arrives on the Island. In the app, Pobb and Professor Aubie are characters in the Island companion experience, alongside other components.

Does Sill work on a Mac without a notch?

Yes. The Island appears at the top of displays with or without a notch. Free and Pro both support display placement and collapsing or expanding; each tool and component follows its own access requirements.

Do files and tools need an internet connection?

The Shelf and local content management work offline. Translate sends submitted text to your selected service, and Weather fetches data online. Files accesses local folders you choose. Clipboard history is encrypted locally and off by default.

Is Sill Pro a subscription?

No. Sill Pro is a one-time purchase with a free 7-day App Store trial that does not renew or charge automatically. Your content and settings remain after the trial, and Free features continue working; Pro components and tools pause. Restore previous purchases in the app.

Which macOS version and account do I need?

Sill requires macOS 14 or later and no Sill account. Downloads, trials, and purchases use your App Store account.

Can I download Sill 2.0 now?

Sill 2.0.0 build 8 is in TestFlight testing. Public TestFlight access and the Mac App Store release have not been announced. The App Store link opens the currently published version.
