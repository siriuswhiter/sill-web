Privacy Policy

Updated October 20, 2026

Sill is local-first: stashed content stays on your Mac and core features work without an account or network connection. Sill does not offer account registration or sign-in. App Store distribution and payments, RevenueCat purchase validation, and Sentry crash diagnostics may connect to third-party services. This policy explains those practices and your choices.

1. Where Your Data Lives

Files, images, snippets, and URLs placed on Sill remain on your Mac. Text, links, small files, and images captured directly from the clipboard are stored in the app sandbox. Larger files remain at their original location by default; Sill stores only a macOS security-scoped bookmark and local metadata so it can access them again. Pinning or locking an item may create a local sandbox copy as part of the action you requested. Sill does not upload your stashed content to purchase or diagnostic services.

2. Purchase Identity and Trial

Sill requires no registration or sign-in. Apple handles trials, purchases, and restoration. RevenueCat associates access with an anonymous App User ID and processes product identifiers, original transaction dates, and purchase status. Sill does not collect sign-in names, email addresses, or third-party login tokens. The local Keychain stores trial timing records to prevent system clock changes from reactivating an expired trial.

3. App Store Purchases

Sill Pro transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store payment credentials such as card or bank details. Sill uses RevenueCat to validate and synchronize purchase entitlements. RevenueCat may process an app user identifier, product identifiers, purchase status, and receipt-related information solely to provide, restore, and support purchased features, not for advertising.

4. Clipboard Access

By default, Sill reads the clipboard when you paste, use a shortcut, or choose a capture command. Pro users can enable clipboard history to record text, URLs, and images in the background. History is encrypted on this Mac and is not uploaded. Exclude apps, choose retention of 1, 7, or 30 days, clear history at any time, or clear it on quit. Capture stops when disabled, when Pro access expires, or when the Mac sleeps; resuming does not collect values from the inactive period.

Share Import and Text Recognition

When you choose to share to Sill from another app, the share extension receives the selected content and passes it to Sill through an encrypted inbox on your Mac. Temporary files used during processing are cleaned up after completion or cancellation. Sharing does not authorize Sill to browse other content in the source app.

When Pro text search in images and PDFs is enabled, Sill extracts text locally and stores an encrypted search index. Files and recognized text are not sent to a cloud service. Sensitive items are excluded; at most the first 20 PDF pages are processed, and oversized or unsupported files may be skipped. You can disable recognition or rebuild the index in Settings.

Connected tools and permissions

Translate sends submitted text and language information to your chosen service. The default MyMemory service needs no API key and receives the text in an HTTPS query parameter. DeepL, Microsoft Translator, Google Cloud, and OpenAI-compatible services use credentials you configure. Model services also receive the selected model and translation instructions. Credentials are stored in the local Keychain; providers process data under their API privacy policies and may retain submitted text or charge separately. If you choose DeepL API Free, its API privacy terms allow submitted text and translations to be processed for a limited period for model training. This does not imply the same policy for DeepL API Pro or other services. Weather uses Apple Weather by default and sends the selected location’s approximate coordinates to Apple. If you explicitly choose Open-Meteo, Sill sends approximate coordinates and your configured commercial Customer API key directly to Open-Meteo. The key is stored in the local Data Protection Keychain and is not synced through iCloud. City search uses Apple geocoding in both modes. Choosing Current Location requests macOS permission, and Sill requests location with about three-kilometer accuracy. Files accesses selected local folders.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual items at any time; normal deletion first moves them to Recently Deleted for 7 days. Free and Pro can both undo immediately or permanently delete after confirmation. Free and Pro can also restore items later during the retention window and enable automatic cleanup for unpinned items after 1 to 365 days. Restoring restarts the retention period. Sill does not offer accounts. You manage local content and clipboard history separately; deleting local content does not delete purchase records held by Apple.

6. Crash and Performance Diagnostics

To find and fix crashes, hangs, and serious storage failures, Sill release builds configured for diagnostics use Sentry to send minimized diagnostic data automatically. This may include crash stack traces, error types and codes, app version and build, macOS version, device architecture, session state, performance and hang metrics, and content-free operation events. After a crash, a report may be stored on the device and sent the next time Sill starts. Sentry also processes the IP address needed to establish the network connection.

We do not intentionally send stashed text, clipboard content, file contents, file names, full file paths, web addresses, email addresses, account IDs, access tokens, or payment credentials to Sentry. Sill disables Sentry's default personal information, network request recording, session replay, screenshots, application logs, and performance tracing. Routine operational failures are retained only as filtered diagnostic context attached to a relevant error report. Diagnostic data is used for product stability, not advertising, cross-app tracking, or user profiling.

7. Service Providers and International Processing

We share data with service providers only as needed for the relevant feature: Apple for app distribution and payments, RevenueCat for trial and purchase entitlement validation, and Sentry for crash and performance diagnostics. These providers may process data outside your country or region subject to their contractual safeguards, security measures, and applicable law. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertising networks.

8. Website Data

This marketing website does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies. It stores only your language preference locally in your browser. Demo videos use YouTube privacy-enhanced embeds and connect to YouTube only after you choose to play. During playback, YouTube may process your IP address, device information, and playback interactions under its privacy policy. Our hosting provider may still process ordinary server logs, such as IP address, request time, and browser information, for security and reliability.

9. Retention and Your Rights

You control local content as described in Section 5. Purchase and diagnostic records are retained only as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, maintain security, or resolve disputes, and are then deleted or anonymized; exact periods may also depend on provider settings and legal requirements. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. Contact support for data-rights requests; we may first need to verify your identity.

10. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy and will post the revised date on this page. Where reasonably practical, material changes will be communicated through the website, an in-app notice, or another appropriate method. For questions or requests about this policy or our handling of personal information, contact support@sill-app.com.
