Privacy Policy

PRIVACY // LAST UPDATED SEPTEMBER 14, 2026

Sill is built around a local-first philosophy: stashed content remains on your Mac, and core features work without an account or network connection. You may optionally sign in with Apple or Google for an account-based trial and entitlement synchronization. Purchase validation, account services, software updates, and crash and performance diagnostics may connect to third-party services. This policy explains those practices and your choices.

1. Where Your Data Lives

Files, images, snippets, and URLs placed on Sill remain on your Mac. Text, links, small files, and images captured directly from the clipboard are stored in the app sandbox. Larger files remain at their original location by default; Sill stores only a macOS security-scoped bookmark and local metadata so it can access them again. Pinning an item for long-term storage or marking it sensitive may create a local sandbox copy as part of the action you requested. Sill does not upload your stashed content to purchase or diagnostic services.

2. Optional Account and Sign-In

Core Sill features and App Store purchases do not require an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, Supabase Auth processes the stable user identifier, name, email address (which may be an Apple private relay address), account creation and recent sign-in times, and session tokens returned through that identity provider. Sill uses the Supabase user UUID—not your email—as the RevenueCat app user identifier to synchronize trials and purchase access. Session tokens are stored in the local Keychain.

3. App Store Purchases

Sill Pro transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store payment credentials such as card or bank details. Sill uses RevenueCat to validate and synchronize purchase entitlements. RevenueCat may process an app user identifier, product identifiers, purchase status, and receipt-related information solely to provide, restore, and support purchased features, not for advertising.

4. Clipboard Access

Sill never polls or snoops on your clipboard continuously. Free users can focus the island and press ⌘V to paste manually. Pro users can also press the “Place clipboard on island” shortcut (⌥⌘C by default) or choose the capture menu command. Sill reads the clipboard once only when one of these explicit actions occurs.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual items at any time; normal deletion first moves them to Recently Deleted for 7 days. Free and Pro can both undo immediately or permanently delete after confirmation. Pro can also restore items later during the retention window and enable automatic cleanup for unpinned items after 1 to 365 days. Restoring restarts the retention period. Signed-in users can permanently delete their Sill account and Supabase account profile in Settings > Account. Account deletion does not delete local stashed content or purchase records held by Apple; local content remains under your separate control.

6. Crash and Performance Diagnostics

To find and fix crashes, hangs, and serious storage failures, Sill release builds configured for diagnostics use Sentry to send minimized diagnostic data automatically. This may include crash stack traces, error types and codes, app version and build, macOS version, device architecture, session state, performance and hang metrics, and content-free operation events. After a crash, a report may be stored on the device and sent the next time Sill starts. Sentry also processes the IP address needed to establish the network connection.

We do not intentionally send stashed text, clipboard content, file contents, file names, full file paths, web addresses, email addresses, account IDs, access tokens, or payment credentials to Sentry. Sill disables Sentry's default personal information, network request recording, session replay, screenshots, application logs, and performance tracing. Routine operational failures are retained only as filtered diagnostic context attached to a relevant error report. Diagnostic data is used for product stability, not advertising, cross-app tracking, or user profiling.

7. Service Providers and International Processing

We share data with service providers only as needed for the relevant feature: Apple and Google for optional sign-in, Supabase for account authentication and deletion, Apple for app distribution and payments, RevenueCat for trial and purchase entitlement validation, and Sentry for crash and performance diagnostics. These providers may process data outside your country or region subject to their contractual safeguards, security measures, and applicable law. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertising networks.

8. Website Data

This marketing website does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies. It stores only your language preference locally in your browser. Our hosting provider may still process ordinary server logs, such as IP address, request time, and browser information, for security and reliability.

9. Retention and Your Rights

You control local content as described in Section 5. Account profile data is retained until you delete the account in the app. Purchase and diagnostic records are retained only as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, maintain security, or resolve disputes, and are then deleted or anonymized; exact periods may also depend on provider settings and legal requirements. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. If you cannot use the in-app deletion control, contact support; we may first need to verify your identity.

10. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy and will post the revised date on this page. Where reasonably practical, material changes will be communicated through the website, an in-app notice, or another appropriate method. For questions or requests about this policy or our handling of personal information, contact support@sill-app.com.
