Common Recovery Steps

  • Deleted something by mistake: Free and Pro can both choose Undo immediately. Pro can also restore it from Recently Deleted within 7 days; every user can permanently delete it after confirmation.
  • A referenced file is missing: If the original moved, was renamed, or lost permission, choose “Locate Referenced File…” and select it again to restore access.
  • A Pro batch partially failed: Open the failure details, check the original files, permissions, and storage location, then choose “Retry Failed Items.” Successful items are not repeated.
  • A Pro edit is not saved yet: When switching items or leaving the editor, choose Save, Don’t Save, or Cancel. If saving fails, Sill keeps the draft so you can retry after resolving the storage issue.
  • A Pro shortcut conflicts: Record it again in Settings → General. If the new combination is unavailable, Sill keeps the previous working shortcut; Free continues to use the default show / hide shortcut.
  • The island is on the wrong display: Pro can choose a target display or Automatic in Settings → General → Island Display; Free uses automatic selection.

Helpful to Include in Your Email

  • Your macOS version and Mac model (e.g. MacBook Pro with notch or Mac mini with external display)
  • The specific workflow when the issue occurred: e.g. dragging in or out, pinning, batch actions, restoring content, locating a referenced file, or clipboard shortcut capture
  • Screenshots or screen recordings are always appreciated

Privacy & Purchase Support

For privacy, account deletion, or purchase restoration questions, use “Privacy,” “Delete Account,” or “Restore Purchase” in the subject line. Core features do not require sign-in, and signed-in users can delete their Sill account in Settings > Account. Never include passwords, Apple ID credentials, or payment card details.

System Requirements

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Sill is a native macOS app that runs as a menu-bar and top-of-screen accessory without occupying Dock space.
