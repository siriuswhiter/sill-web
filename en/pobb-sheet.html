Four scales of liquid glass

Each is like a held drop of water—flowing slowly inside, refracting the light around it. Not decoration, but how Pobb senses the world.

  1. 01SenseThe top scale turns first toward cursors, sounds, and new arrivals.
  2. 02ShelterAt rest they fold into a blanket that just covers his face.
  3. 03MemoryTheir light briefly keeps nearby colors—never the contents.
  4. 04FeelingThey glow tail-to-shoulder when happy, flare together when startled.
