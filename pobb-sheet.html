04 / 护鳞04 / SCALES

液态玻璃的四枚护鳞Four scales of liquid glass

每一枚都像凝住的一滴水，内部缓慢流动、折射着身边的光。它们不是装饰，而是泡泡感知世界的方式。Each is like a held drop of water—flowing slowly inside, refracting the light around it. Not decoration, but how Pobb senses the world.

  1. 01感知Sense最上方的一枚最先转向光标、声音与新落下的东西。The top scale turns first toward cursors, sounds, and new arrivals.
  2. 02守护Shelter睡觉时逐片合上，变成一床刚好遮住脸的小被子。At rest they fold into a blanket that just covers his face.
  3. 03记忆Memory水光短暂留下身边事物的颜色，却从不读取内容。Their light briefly keeps nearby colors—never the contents.
  4. 04情绪Feeling开心时由尾到肩依次亮起，受惊时同时张开。They glow tail-to-shoulder when happy, flare together when startled.
