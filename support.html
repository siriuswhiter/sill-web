常见恢复方法 Common Recovery Steps

  • 误删内容：删除后可立即点“撤销”，或到资料库的“最近删除”中恢复。内容会保留 7 天；永久删除需再次确认且无法撤销。
  • Deleted something by mistake: Choose Undo immediately, or restore it later from Recently Deleted in the library. Items remain there for 7 days; permanent deletion requires confirmation and cannot be undone.
  • 引用文件找不到：原文件被移动、改名或权限失效时，选择“重新定位引用文件…”并选中原文件，即可恢复访问。
  • A referenced file is missing: If the original moved, was renamed, or lost permission, choose “Locate Referenced File…” and select it again to restore access.
  • 批量操作部分失败：打开失败详情检查原文件、访问权限和存储位置，然后选择“重试失败项”；已成功的项目不会重复执行。
  • A batch partially failed: Open the failure details, check the original files, permissions, and storage location, then choose “Retry Failed Items.” Successful items are not repeated.
  • 编辑尚未保存：切换内容或离开编辑器时，选择保存、放弃或取消。若保存失败，Sill 会保留草稿；修复存储问题后可再次保存。
  • An edit is not saved yet: When switching items or leaving the editor, choose Save, Don’t Save, or Cancel. If saving fails, Sill keeps the draft so you can retry after resolving the storage issue.
  • 快捷键冲突：在“设置 → 通用”重新录制“显示 / 隐藏置物岛”和“将剪贴板放到岛上”。若新组合无效，Sill 会保留之前可用的快捷键。
  • A shortcut conflicts: In Settings → General, record new shortcuts for “Show / hide island” and “Place clipboard on island.” If a new combination is unavailable, Sill keeps the previous working shortcut.
  • 置物岛出现在错误屏幕：在“设置 → 通用 → 置物岛显示器”选择目标显示器或“自动选择”。已选显示器断开时，Sill 会暂时自动选择，重新连接后继续使用已保存的选择。
  • The island is on the wrong display: In Settings → General → Island Display, choose a display or Automatic. If the saved display disconnects, Sill temporarily falls back to automatic selection and keeps your preference for when it returns.

反馈时建议提供的信息 Helpful to Include in Your Email

  • 您的 macOS 系统版本（如 macOS 14.5 或 macOS 15.0）以及 Mac 硬件型号（MacBook Pro 刘海屏或 Mac mini / 外接显示器）
  • Your macOS version and Mac model (e.g. MacBook Pro with notch or Mac mini with external display)
  • 发生异常时的具体操作流程：如文件拖入、拖出、长存固定、批量操作、恢复内容、重新定位文件或按全局快捷键抓取剪贴板
  • The specific workflow when the issue occurred: e.g. dragging in or out, pinning, batch actions, restoring content, locating a referenced file, or clipboard shortcut capture
  • 若有屏幕录屏或截图更佳
  • Screenshots or screen recordings are always appreciated
